📄 About CampusCoin

Welcome to CampusCoin!

CampusCoin transforms how South African students stretch their monthly budgets. We gamify smart spending to help you save more while earning grocery rewards.

How It Works

  • Track Spending: Log your essential expenses (data, electricity, food)
  • Earn Instantly: Get 10% back in grocery rewards on every transaction
  • Build Loyalty: Reach 12 months of consistent spending to unlock R6,000 grocery vouchers
  • Save Smart: Use rewards to offset future expenses and stretch your budget

Why CampusCoin?

Student life means tight budgets. CampusCoin rewards responsible spending habits, helping you build financial discipline while immediately reducing expenses through grocery savings.

The Community

Join thousands of South African students using CampusCoin to manage their finances smarter. Every transaction contributes to your journey toward financial freedom.
